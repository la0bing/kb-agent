# Undergraduate Student Handbook

## Course Registration

Course registration opens two weeks before the semester begins and closes at
the end of Week 2. Adding or dropping a subject after Week 2 requires the
approval of the faculty dean and incurs a late processing fee of RM50. A
student may carry a maximum of 21 credit hours in a normal semester and 12
credit hours in a short semester.

## Attendance Requirement

A student must attend at least 80% of the lectures, tutorials and laboratory
sessions for every registered course. A student who falls below this level is
barred from sitting the final examination for that course and receives a grade
of F. Medical certificates from a registered clinic are the only accepted
grounds for excused absence.

## Examination Results

Results are published on the student portal four weeks after the final
examination period ends. A student whose CGPA falls below 2.00 is placed on
academic probation for the following semester and must meet an academic
advisor before registering. Two consecutive semesters below 2.00 lead to
dismissal from the programme.

## PTPTN And Fees

Tuition fees are due before the end of Week 3 of each semester. PTPTN
disbursement is credited directly to the university and any balance is paid
to the student bank account within six weeks of the semester start. A student
with outstanding fees is blocked from registering for the next semester.

## Kolej Kediaman

Hostel applications open three weeks before the semester begins and are
allocated by distance from campus and CGPA. Residents must vacate their room
within three days of their last examination. Cooking in rooms is prohibited
and repeated breaches lead to withdrawal of residency.
